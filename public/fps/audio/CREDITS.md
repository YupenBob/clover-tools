# FPS practice audio

These recordings are distributed under CC0-1.0:

- **The Free Firearm Sound Library** — Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney. AK-47 `C_28P.wav` and AR-15 `D_32P.wav`. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
- **Gun reload sounds** — SpringySpringo. `assaultriflereload1_0.wav`, recorded using airsoft guns. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds

License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Local edits trim individual transients, convert to mono 44.1 kHz PCM, remove very low frequencies, normalize level and fade tails. See `manifest.json` for source and derivative hashes and exact processing. The M4A1-S/Phantom sounds use filtered AR-15 recordings; Vandal uses a processed AK recording. These are original training sound designs, **not recordings of those games or verified replicas of their weapon sounds**. Hit confirmations are synthesized locally.
